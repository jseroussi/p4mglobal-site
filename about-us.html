About Us

Established in 2008 and headquartered in South Florida, P4M Global provides executive-level advisory and consulting services to financial entities, fintech companies, and payment processors. We specialize in fintech platform architecture, advanced data analytics, and Agentic AI — helping organizations architect scalable technology ecosystems and build automated, data-driven reporting layers.

Our practice is led by an executive with over four decades of leadership across financial processing, telecommunications, and large-scale technology infrastructure. As Acting CEO/CTO of a cross-border virtual banking and card services company, our founder has designed and operated end-to-end data analytics platforms, built PCI Level 1 processing environments, and architected integrations with issuing banks, card processors, and payment gateways.

We bring a rare combination of C-suite strategic vision and hands-on technical execution — from Power BI dashboard design and SQL data modeling to Agentic AI workflow automation. P4M Global is not a generalist consulting firm: we are a specialized advisory practice built on deep domain expertise in financial technology and data.
